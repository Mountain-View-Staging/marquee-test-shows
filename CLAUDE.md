# Marquee test shows — PUBLIC

Everything in this repository is public. Read README.md's rules before adding anything: generic
content only, generated media and Inter 4.1 under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (its licence in
`LICENSES/`) only, no real show codes, no live service URLs, no internal identifiers. CI runs a
leak scan on every push, and the Pages deploy runs it again before it publishes.

The shows and the style book are generated by a tool outside this repository and committed;
never hand-edit either. Everything under `shows/` and `brands/`, the `.db` files in `legacy/`,
and `LICENSES/Inter-OFL-1.1.txt` (the font release's own licence, copied verbatim) are
generated; the README, the scripts, the workflows and `legacy/README.md` /
`legacy/expected.json` are written by hand.

- `brands/example/example-2026/1/` is a stand-in brand portal: a style book in the branding
  specification's §7.2 layout, which `BRAND26` imports. Its twelve font files are Inter 4.1,
  unmodified, and they — with their copies in `shows/BRAND26/` — are under the OFL, not this
  repository's licence. A published style version never changes its files: the generator
  rewrites it byte for byte.
- To reset: `scripts/reset.sh`. To check a checkout: `python3 scripts/leak-scan.py`,
  `scripts/verify.sh`, `scripts/budget.sh`, and `scripts/load-cartridges.mjs` with a checkout of
  the Cartridge Specification.
- A test copies a show before opening it; `scripts/lock.sh` makes the authoring databases
  read-only so nothing opens one here in place.
- The leak scan's patterns live in `scripts/leak-scan.py` alone (the scan skips that file). Real
  codes, event names and brand names are listed there as SHA-256 digests, never as text, and a finding is
  reported by position, never by its text: CI logs are public too.
